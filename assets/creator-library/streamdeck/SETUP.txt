Producer Sounds — Stream Deck setup

1. Install the Stream Deck desktop app on the computer running Producer.
2. Open ai.boomin.producer.sounds.streamDeckPlugin to install Producer Sounds.
3. In Producer, enter your room and open the Sounds panel.
4. Choose Stream Deck → Show pairing connection → Copy connection.
5. Drag Producer Sounds → Play sound onto a hardware key.
6. Paste the connection into the action settings, click Connect, then choose a room sound.
7. Add Stop all sounds to another key. Stream Deck+ users can add Effects volume to a dial.

Pairing is remembered on that computer and limited to localhost. Dev and production
use separate connections. Reconnect once when moving from dev to production or to
another computer. Do not share the pairing connection or export it publicly.

The plugin follows the active room. Sound assignments use content IDs, so moving
or renaming pads keeps the hardware assignment. Assign the sound to each room
where you want to use it. Missing assignments produce an alert instead of playing
a different sound.

You can also enable global shortcuts in Producer's Sounds panel:
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+1 through 9: first nine pads; Ctrl+Alt+Shift+0: stop all.
On macOS, Alt is Option. These work while Producer is in the background.

Sounds play into recording, streaming, and the guest-return mix. Preview is
headphones-only. 'Hear effects' controls monitoring of live effects. Use DJ for
long music tracks; the effects player accepts clips up to 60 seconds.

Playback modes: restart, play/stop, overlap, loop/stop. Effects have individual
volume, fade and color. Stop all is immediate. Imported files outside the permanent
sound folder are copied into Music/Producer/Sounds. Removing a pad removes the room
assignment, not its library file. Your original collection is in:
Music/Producer/Sounds/Myinstants-US-Top-250

No user sound files or pairing credentials are included in this plugin.
