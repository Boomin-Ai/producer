01 — Live

Every stage at once, from your desk.

Producer embeds the same battle-tested engine OBS is built on. Capture your screen and camera, encode once on your GPU, and send the stream to every platform simultaneously — no cloud relay taking a cut of your bitrate or your wallet.

ONE ENCODE

Hardware-encoded, fanned out

One NVENC or VideoToolbox encode, delivered to every ingest at full quality. Your GPU does the work once, not per platform.

NO MIDDLEMAN

Desktop → platform, direct

Your machine connects straight to Twitch, YouTube, and Kick ingest servers. No relay in the middle, nothing to subscribe to, no cap on destinations.

LOCKED DOWN

Keys stay in your keychain

Stream keys go into your OS keychain once, and are never readable back out — not by the UI, not by us. Open source means you can check.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Is it actually free?
Yes. The app is AGPL-3.0 open source and every feature in it is free. Boomin's hosted backend has a free tier; self-hosting on Cloudflare costs $0 for a normal creator's workload. There is no pro plan.
Does my stream go through your servers?
No. Producer's engine connects from your desktop directly to each platform's ingest servers. There is no relay in the middle — which is also why there's nothing for us to charge you for.
What platforms does it run on?
macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows, and Linux. Live multistreaming ships on macOS today and is in the works for Windows; posting and scheduling work everywhere.
Do I need an account?
Only if you use Boomin's hosted backend for posting. Going live needs no account at all — paste your stream keys and go. Self-hosters never touch Boomin's servers.
How do you make money then?
Producer is free and stays free. Boomin sells a commercial SDK for companies that want this publishing stack inside their own products — that funds the open-source work.
