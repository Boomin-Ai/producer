# Producer 0.4.86

Native guest audio reaches the mixer meter, host monitoring, and the recording mix. Guests who stay onstage and unmuted remain audible when a Set layout switches to another camera.

Verified with a phone guest and a Mac using LiveKit in the Kleveland Set. The Phone mute worked, the phone heard the Mac, and a local recording captured the phone mic while its camera was out of the shot.

Guests may still need to refresh the browser after the host leaves and reopens the room.
